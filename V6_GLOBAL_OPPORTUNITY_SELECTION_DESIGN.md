# NBOT V6 — Global Coin × Strategy Opportunity Selection
**Owner-approved research design — 2026-10-18.** **DESIGN / OFFLINE RESEARCH ONLY.** No new VPS jobs, paper/live orders, automatic promotions, V5 edits, default-branch merges, or changes to already-running studies are authorized by this document.

## 1. What the owner wants (binding scope)
Every shared completed 5-minute decision candle, examine the **same fixed 25 eligible Binance USDT-M coins independently under each of the 8 new strategy families**, considering LONG and SHORT only where that family's causal entry signal is genuinely valid. Produce up to **25 × 8 = 200 coin/family evaluation cells**, potentially **400 coin/family/side forecast slots**. **Eight estimates per coin** means each family is independently considered; it does **not** mean eight guaranteed valid entries or eight calibrated probabilities at every instant.

At each time, estimate a **probability distribution of executable net returns conditional on a specific entry, direction, exit policy and portfolio state**; compare **all coins and families in the same timestamp**, select **exactly one best *qualified* global opportunity** when the slot is empty, or **NO TRADE**. Rank by cost- and risk-adjusted *expected outcome*, never simply highest win probability and never historical realized future profit.

**Core study:** eight independent native Freqtrade strategy families: `ema_cross`, `donchian_breakout`, `bollinger_reversion`, `rsi_reversion`, `macd_momentum`, `stochastic_reversal`, `atr_breakout`, `volume_trend`. **Additional frozen controls / optional expanded challenger:** original `breakout` and `pullback` are two distinct original setup families. This gives an *optional* 25×10×2 ceiling of 500, to be compared as a separate registered experiment, not silently substituted for the owner's **25×8** base design. No family is automatically dropped based on exploratory losses; report all results and allow the owner to choose after reviewing evidence.

- Pure offline `scripts/m2r_v6_global_opportunity_ranker.py` with `choose_global_opportunity`, risk gates, one-slot and NO TRADE behavior; **synthetic** tests in `tests/test_m2r_v6_global_opportunity_ranker.py`.

The existing pure ranker must not be described as a finished AI system. Historical native results alone cannot train a portfolio-level winner without reconstructing the **candidate and no-candidate universe**.

## 3. Single-decision lifecycle
1. **As-of market snapshot:** 25 contracts as historically eligible, last *completed* 5m candle, BTC/regime context, 1h candles only if complete, frozen common exchange/cost/fee assumptions, available capital and one global open-slot state. Never use later listings, later volume ranks, later 5m highs, funding announcements unavailable at entry, or today's pair whitelist for a backtest's earlier date.
2. **Candidate generation:** run each of the 8 independently frozen technical entry conditions against each eligible coin and supported side. Preserve no-signal cells and explicit rejection reasons. Permit different families to signal the same coin and direction; treat as separately labeled experiments, not eight independent statistical observations.
3. **Causal prediction:** at time `t`, use **already fitted** LightGBM family/pair (or shared conditional family-aware) model version with training cutoff and label-observation embargo before `t`. For each valid (coin,family,side,exit_policy), return calibrated `P(net>0)`, `E(net return)`, downside-loss metric and uncertainty. Store forecast-generation time, model ID/version, training cutoff, calibration version and data hash. If unavailable or stale, record `UNSCORABLE` and exclude from ranking, not assume 0 or 50%.
4. **Comparable objective:** model the **same configured exit policy and realistic horizon** across competitors. `net = gross Freqtrade native executed PnL - missing execution costs`; account for native fee, funding, spread, slippage and sensitivity to additional 10/15/25bp round-trip execution stress, **without double counting**. Do not compare a +5% fixed ROI target model with a trailing-exit model unless policy-conditioned return distributions and horizons are made comparable. Apply uncertainty and downside reserve to expected after-cost value; explicit risk and minimal-edge thresholds are **predeclared/tuned using earlier data only**.
5. **One global choice:** freeze one timestamp-wide snapshot; deterministic rank with tie-breaking; enforce empty global slot, no overexposure, cooldown, min liquidity, safe volatility and exchange eligibility; choose best qualified (coin,family,side,policy) or `NO_TRADE`. Occupied slot means no extra entry (can evaluate rejected alternatives for diagnostics without pretending simultaneous fills).
6. **Actual portfolio simulation:** pass the chosen timed candidate to a **single native Freqtrade full-engine 25-pair chronological portfolio**, with one 1× $1,000 nominal slot initially (consistent with historical baseline), protections and coherent exit state, future-bar stop/ROI/trailing behavior, funding and modeled transaction costs. Model predictions are frozen **before** outcomes are simulated. No stitching profits from separately backtested strategies and no homemade standalone execution accounting as substitute for native Freqtrade.
7. **Learning and review:** once each candidate's complete future evaluation horizon becomes observable, record *counterfactual historical candle-based* labels also for rejected candidates; distinguish from actually filled portfolio trades. Correlated candidate outcomes from the same 5m bar share time groups for training/evaluation. NO TRADE is a real recorded option with zero traded return and opportunity-cost diagnostics, not a guaranteed positive strategy.

## 5. Profit protection: evaluate fairly without future advantage
Reuse the existing fixed-2% stop / +5% target baseline, existing trailing A/B, and the queued **five new exit-policy × eight strategies × three periods** native trials. Do **not** rerun completed trials.
- Extract pre-exit maximum favorable/adverse excursions conservatively from already complete 5m candles; explicitly audit any within-candle sequence ambiguity and gap/slippage failures.
- Explore locks in the vicinity of **+1%, +2%, +3%** rather than promising those exact net fills; record early exits that sacrifice later +5% opportunities and how freed portfolio capital changes subsequent selection.
- Run **two separate ranker comparisons**: (A) frozen common existing exit policy; (B) a profit-protection policy selected on earlier training/validation data, then frozen. Only after these may a policy-conditioned chooser select among exit policies as an **optional challenger**, to avoid multiplying lookahead/selection bias.
- Compare both gross and net win rates, expectancy, average win/loss, drawdown, fees and lower-tail risk. A higher win rate alone does not pass.

## 6. Evaluation plan: staged, reusable, cost-aware
**Prerequisite 0 (reuse and preserve):** finish existing isolated library service; preserve model/ZIP hashes and detailed 8-strategy native + trailing results; let the already-armed *finite* profit-protection successor run only if its safety checks pass. Verify successor state. Historical Jan–Sep native 30/60/90/180 **2,475 fits** completed; Stage2 original 25-only as-of diagnostic recovery exited 0; **true broad-market 25-of-N economic replay remains unperformed**. These are not new tasks to duplicate. Never start competing Docker jobs or pull a new checkout into an active runner.
**A — Data/provenance audit:** inventory all native fills, original baseline, candidate signal code/versions, 5m/1h/funding and usable chronological spans. Quantify missing depth/spread; quarantine suspect label/lookahead windows. Emit frozen cohort/target/exit-policy manifest. Every future evaluation must use actual historical listings, not current eligibility hindsight.
**B — Opportunity matrix:** generate all eight strategies' 25×side candidate/no-signal snapshots chronologically for common dates. Check no future data in features, multi-family signal timing, market selection and latency; use hard negative examples and no-signal abstentions.
**C — Candidate model experiment:** fit comparable family/side-conditional net-return distribution predictors and calibrated probability; test independent family models versus shared family-aware model as predeclared alternatives. For each training period (30/60/90/180 days where enough warmup exists), use purged grouped chronological walk-forward with label horizon+execution delay embargo; forbid model trained on July/Aug/Sep for evaluation earlier in those months. Keep calibrated model scoring on genuine later data, and retain null baseline.
**D — Global ranker/portfolio:** integrate the pure selector through one isolated Freqtrade-compatible research adapter, re-simulate one slot chronologically and reconcile all native trades against trade ledger, fees, exits and position occupancy. Compare eligible candidates at identical timestamps before entries. Never use an after-the-fact best-of-eight portfolio made by combining independent runs.
**E — Challenger matrix:** matched-date comparisons: original current FreqAI V6, no-AI rule baseline, each of eight single-family policies, 8h regime router, raw probability-only chooser, net-expectancy-only chooser, risk-adjusted chooser, risk-adjusted + profit-lock, NO TRADE, optional original 2-family expanded contest, optional PPO separately. Report fees/slippage sensitivity, drawdown, return, Calmar/Sortino where stable, exposure, turnover, accuracy/calibration, pair/regime concentration and confidence intervals with time-block bootstrap (not independent trade assumptions).
**F — True unseen decision:** July/Aug/Sep 2026 have been repeatedly inspected; they are exploration/replication **not pristine blind proof**. Reserve the **next genuinely uninspected future interval** with a frozen protocol and source hashes, and do not re-optimize after examining that report. A strategy with negative risk-adjusted aftercost portfolio performance vs NO TRADE stays rejected, even if its classifier accuracy looks good.

## 8. Integration and safety acceptance tests before scaling
- Matrix cardinality 25×8×2, all 25 coins considered, no entry signal means no eligible candidate; required 5m synchronized snapshot and deterministic tie breaks.
- Valid LONG/SHORT comparison and rejection of missing contract history, future timestamps, future training labels, mismatched targets/policy, model drift, high uncertainty, low liquidity and occupied slot.
- Price path labeled only after observation period; NO TRADE if every candidate fails, even when highest `P(win)` is superficially high; rank according to net risk-adjusted economics.
- Freqtrade native end-to-end one-slot test: simultaneity and opening/closing timing, trailing intrabar ambiguity, freed-capital reentry, max position and fees/funding reconciled.
- Walk-forward leakage/embargo and calibration tests with regime shift, correlated candidates and strategy duplication. Independent run artifacts and no data snooping.
- Resource trial on 8GB/2vCPU VPS using **small pilot** before full 25×8 inference/training; RAM/CPU and Docker isolation; no data from private credentials.
- Enforce no-paper/no-live/no-auto-promotion, independent cost sensitivities, finite process with restartable checkpoints; existing running jobs never interrupted.
