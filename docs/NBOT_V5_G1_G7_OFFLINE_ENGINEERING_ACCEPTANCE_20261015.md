## Engineering blockers requiring actual code work (not just market time)

1. **Original after-cost G4→G5 handoff:** `ContinuousResearch.tick()` produces source-verified *gross price paths*, then records `economic_state=HELD_PENDING_INDEPENDENT_EXIT_QUOTE_FEE_SLIPPAGE_SETTLED_FUNDING`. It does not invoke `assess_quote_constrained_net()` or automatically construct signed accepted economic exports. `accept_verified_export()` currently requires separately supplied reviewer-signed original-grade evidence. **Simply starting the services cannot complete the G5 dataset.**
2. **Sustained 50-coin archival:** compressed RAM/checkpoints cap at 8 GiB but do not yet have proven safe hash-preserving rollover/off-host verified retention. G4 streaming pass over large compressed files can still be repeated for many pending hypotheses. Implement an indexed, bounded multi-intent replay or source-window archiving without treating omitted bytes as verified stop/target evidence.
3. **G5→G6→G7 operational research:** evaluators, training primitives and locked paper bookkeeping exist but reviewed qualification, time-separated Champion/Challenger generation, provenance-locked deployment and model handoff are not one independently tested unattended pipeline. Explicitly reject synthetic grade in production.
4. **G7 roadmap reconciliation:** authoritative v1.1 §§17–26 provide a first qualified Champion in a single paper portfolio before second independently qualified Challenger exists. Current unlock requires both. Resolve with an owner-reviewed, fail-closed implementation; do not silently reduce authentication.
5. **Controlled deployment prerequisites:** finalize a prospectively declared G4 policy/activation, frozen original G1 cohort manifest, G5/G6 protocol, approved source SHA, resource budgeting and services' source preflights BEFORE enabling observation. The installed pin is deliberately stale for safety, not a defect to bypass.
