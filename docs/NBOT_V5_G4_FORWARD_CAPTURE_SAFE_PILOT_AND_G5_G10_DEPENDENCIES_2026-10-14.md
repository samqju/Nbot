## A. Implemented and tested in the separate G4 branch

New additions beyond previously tested original Binance daily archive ZIP and one-off genuine historical/quote/funding source acquisition:

1. `nbot/v5/g4_forward_stream_contract.py`: fixed original public-only 2026 endpoint routing, top-50 frozen cohort, explicit separate **public** combined `@bookTicker`, **market** combined `@aggTrade`, and **market** `@markPrice@1s` streams. Source event/arrival clocks, source epoch, stream/coin and USD-M `st=1`, raw price/qty, aggregate and underlying IDs, indicative funding/mark-price validation. Raw source messages are NOT executed fills or a settlement funding charge.
2. `nbot/v5/g4_forward_vault.py`: standalone private source-only SQLite database, append-only source/cohort tables, raw wire-message receipts, immutable triggers, SHA256 linking each raw frame and source state to preceding receipt, explicit reconnect epoch, unclean-restart/gap HOLD and strict frozen cohort. Refuses production G1/G2/G3/F0, mixed G4 label, V4 and unknown SQLite schemas. Restart and WAL-safe SQLite backup/restore mock proofs. Re-running existing original held stream will **not** turn a rejected gap into approved source.
3. `nbot/v5/g4_forward_capture_runner.py`: a bounded, optional, public-only live WSS receiver, **dry-run/no-network by default**. Requires a 50-symbol original cohort + reproducible cohort SHA manifest, existing separately created private directory with exact G4-only filename, operator `--enable-network`, finite `--max-seconds <= 3600`, `--max-frames <= 100000`, `--max-db-mib <= 256`. It never makes account requests, has no API keys, trading permission, private endpoints, model inference or training functions. Explicit source errors/limits are surfaced and do not constitute verified data continuity. Three independent route connections are needed; an abrupt disconnect creates an UNKNOWN continuity interval.
4. `nbot/v5/g4_prospective_policy.py`: prospective pure research policy template must be SHA-frozen at least five minutes before a later aligned event and effective no later than event OPEN. At original candidate decision, source-known BTC/coin features and causal original bid/ask witness derive side-correct risk geometry (LONG uses ask and stop below; SHORT uses bid and stop above), fixed R target and immutable per-candidate SHA; rejecting later quotes/future regime, unrealized or missing ATR, large spread, invalid source and unsupported modes. Emits only `GEOMETRY_RECORDED`/HOLD, no fill and zero outcomes. The synthetic fixture uses 4-hour horizon, 1.5x ATR stop-distance, 2R target, 0.5% minimum risk, <=1s quote age **as a PROPOSED INITIAL RESEARCH BASELINE, not an accepted deployed policy or optimized strategy**. This must receive independent source-grade and economic-review before prospective research operation.
