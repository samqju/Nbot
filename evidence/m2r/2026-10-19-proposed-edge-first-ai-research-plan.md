**19 October 2026. ## Executive finding

The research framework runs reliably, but the original eight 5m technical strategy families were consistently unprofitable under a +5% target, −2% stop, mandatory 60m time exit, and hypothetical extra 25bp stress. Removing the fixed time exit removed enormous churn and made all 16 monthly comparisons less negative, yet **none of 16 continuous July–September 2026 backtests positive after the extra 25bp stress**. The global AI ranker was trained against a fixed +5%/−2%/60m proxy and a hard positive-class probability 50% gate plus risk penalties, rather than joint coin-strategy-exit expected value, and abstained from all trades. Further stop tweaks **cannot repair unproven entry edge**. Historical July–September has been examined many times, making it inappropriate for final OOS performance claims.

## Question and financial unit definitions

Aim to find a positive **expected net return per executed $1,000 notional position** after exchange fees, realistic pair-specific liquidity/limit-vs-market slippage and funding. This must translate to a positive true 1-position native **portfolio** return and acceptable drawdown, not only better individual hypothetical opportunity labels.

A user request for +0.1% should be stated precisely: +0.1% **net per executed position** is about $1 on the $1,000 notional stake, whereas +0.1% of a simulated $100,000 wallet is $100. On prior modeled costs (0.05% per side native fees + 0.25% roundtrip extra stress), the assumed transaction drag totals ~0.35% before funding, so gross price gains of ~0.45% are required for +0.1% stressed net of notional. Stress is hypothetical, not measured actual spread; do not equate it to factual broker fill costs. This is an accounting hurdle, not a guarantee or target for AI.

## Recommended protocol (priority order)

1. **Freeze original eight entries and all July–Sep results as exploratory.** No grid search of 5/3/2% ROI, 1/2% stop, or 0.1% targets until the intended target and heldout data are predetermined. Verify data continuity, delisting/universe selection, funding-mark alignment, and Freqtrade official lookahead-analysis / recursive-analysis for indicator shifts. Native 5m stop-first same-candle assumptions may favor one stop width.
2. **Construct an honest event-by-event trade path library** for every coin×family×eligible LONG/SHORT at each completed candle, including rejected signals. For each path, label the FIRST TOUCH under conservative prices (stop before target when same candle ambiguous) of −1%, −2%, +1%, +2%, +3%, +5% plus trailing activations +1%, +1.5%, +2% and unbounded/max-duration portfolios. Report maximum favorable/adverse excursion, time to favorable target, protective exit, realistic per-pair costs, funding, and time capital is occupied. Each label matches a precisely defined native Freqtrade policy; training may use future outcomes only as LABELS and purged splits, not as features or ranking inputs.
3. **Start with a simple, auditable non-AI baseline**: identify setups whose aftercost expected net payoff and payoff distribution are better than random-trading/matched-frequency and NO TRADE across older walk-forward folds. Try both original 5m and slower 15m/1h decision horizons to reduce trading cost sensitivity. If no prospectively measurable entry advantage exists, say so; do not train LightGBM to manufacture alpha from weak labels.
4. **Train an exit-aware meta-selector**, not a one-target price predictor: given coin, setup, direction, BTC/market trend, volatility/ATR, volume/liquidity and time-of-day, predict a probabilistic distribution over realizable favorable/adverse excursion, net P&L, duration and drawdown for each allowed exit policy. With only as-of data, compute expected net return and tail risk/capital slot occupancy for each option; compare all 25×8×eligible sides×exits each candle, choose one opportunity or NO TRADE.
5. **Stop imposing an arbitrary ≥50% positive-trade gate** when payoff asymmetry and calibrated expected net outcomes matter. Calibrate selected expected net outcomes on earlier data, penalize uncertainty based on historical forecasting error without eliminating every signal by construction. Risk limits still required; never relax thresholds simply to force selection in repeatedly examined July–September.
6. **Require a genuinely unseen future period**, ideally October onward with historical data downloaded into an isolated new directory, never used to design gate/exit/model settings. If the holdout window is too short for reliable confidence bounds, continue collecting forward observations before accepting evidence. Use purged chronological splits before the holdout and no revisiting the selected settings after looking at Oct. Test native 1-position $1k strategy using actual fee tiers, funding, modeled realistic variable costs, fixed trade count and stress sensitivities. Include statistical uncertainty (block/day bootstrap), max drawdown, number of trades, median hold and forced-close boundary caveats.
7. **Predeclare go/no-go**: positive net *economic* result vs NO TRADE and matched baseline on truly untouched data, positive expected net return per trade with adequate trades and material liquidity, acceptable max drawdown, no lookahead, no paper/live permission until owner inspection. Failure to meet this is a legitimate negative study; keep all losers and diagnostics.
