## 3. COST MODEL: MARKET/TRADE-SPECIFIC HISTORICAL FUNDING ADDED; REAL FILL GATE STILL FAILS

The former target estimated net outcome using per-side 0.05% fees, per-side 0.05% hypothetical slippage, and a constant **0.01% = 1bp funding charge** for every imagined trade. This is not a faithful per-position cost: Binance historical funding occurs at **actual discrete settlement events**, varies by pair and time, and its signed cash flow depends on direction (long pays positive rate; short receives). Implemented [event-funded hypothetical label module](../../scripts/m2r_event_cost_labels.py) and [offline 25-pair label-cost audit](../../scripts/m2r_event_funding_label_audit.py). Uses public archived event rates and **last COMPLETED 1h mark candle's close** as a settlement mark proxy. Event inclusion `next_entry_open < event_time <= hypothetical_exit_candle_close`. Flags unknown intrabar stop/settlement boundary order rather than declaring exact funding. All such future event values are attached to the **supervised target `&-trade_net_return` only**, not any `%-` decision-time features; unknown trailing 12 future label rows remain NaN. Fee/slippage are explicitly still proxies.

Executed the event-funded label comparison for all 25 previously examined Oct 8–15 markets (not the pre-registered blind May–Aug):
- **7,732 overlapping historical hypothetical breakout/pullback opportunities**, not filled orders, not 7,732 wallet trades.
- The original flat 1bp estimate labeled **2,563** positive; historic signed event estimate labeled **2,605** positive; **43** hypothetical labels changed nonpositive→positive, **1** positive→nonpositive. There were **994 settlement event crossings across overlapping hypothetical labels** and **53 boundary-ambiguous outcomes** needing conservative handling.
- Under *additional assumed* roundtrip execution penalties, event-adjusted hypothetical positives: 0bps=2,605; +5bps=2,377; +10bps=2,179; +15bps=2,023; +25bps=1,748. These are NOT real fill-cost results or portfolio returns.
- Historical 1h mark **candle close** is not exact funding settlement mark or observed bid/ask. Stop-market intrabar position timing unknown. The earlier observed event-rate distribution included **208/3,375 events greater than the flat 1bp reserve**, maximum **18.5769bps**. These sources materially improve *label funding assumptions* but cannot prove true spread, impact, market order/stop fills or exchange funding billing.

New [research-only FreqAI challenger strategy](../../user_data/strategies/NbotV6TradeOutcomeFundingResearch.py) and [gated config](../../user_data/config.m2r-funded-purged-research.json) use event-funded targets on native LightGBM with the optional internal embargo. **Actual native BTC/ETH smoke** passed under clean v2 identifier with valid saved models (BTC zero removed, ETH one removed).
